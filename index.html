Shorts Uploader

Shorts Uploader is a personal command-line tool. Its developer uses it to manage videos on YouTube channels that the developer runs. It is not a public service and has no other users.

The tool runs on the developer's own computer and does the following through the YouTube API:

  • uploads videos as private, sets their thumbnails, and schedules them for publication;
  • lists the channel's existing uploads, to avoid uploading the same video twice;
  • reads the channel's comments and viewing statistics, to see how each video performed.

There is no sign-up, no hosted version, and nothing to download here.
